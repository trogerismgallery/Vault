TROGERISM PHOTOGRAPHY

Tom Rogers
Bognor Regis, UK

Trogerism is a philosophy of observation. It is my name, paired with the ism that defines my perspective, the idea that in every photograph, you are seeing a part of my experience, even if I remain unseen.

I spent 8 years traveling around the country for work, and when COVID began in 2020, I started using my phone to document the places I encountered. That period of constant movement became my training ground, I learned to adapt quickly to new environments, whether coastal landscapes, quiet towns, or the intensity of city centers. I am no longer traveling for work, but those years of movement deeply shaped how I see the world today. Around 2023, I began using my first Canon camera, and I have been using Canon ever since to refine my craft.

I am often drawn to the timelessness of black and white, using it to strip away the noise and focus on pure form, emotion, and the geometry of a scene. My approach is defined by spontaneity, I move through my environment scanning for those unique intersections of architecture, light, and human behavior, capturing them in the moment I see them. While I have the patience to wait for a scene to align when the shot demands it, my work is primarily a narrative driven exploration, capturing the world exactly as I observe it, one frame at a time.
